# Candidates — Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest

Competition: `u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046`
Brief: one short angry rant, at most 400 characters.
Eight original candidates were drafted in-session; no stock or circulating jokes used.

## Candidate 1 (289 characters)

The self-checkout has accused me of theft four times today, all because I dared to place my own bag in the bagging area. I am being supervised by a scale with trust issues while a human employee watches from six feet away, arms crossed, radiating the energy of a lifeguard who cannot swim.

## Candidate 2 (268 characters)

Whoever designed the hotel shower deserves a trial. Two knobs, zero labels, and a temperature range that jumps from arctic meltwater to lobster bisque with a quarter turn. I did not shower this morning; I negotiated a hostage release where the hostage was my own skin.

## Candidate 3 (258 characters)

The automatic faucet at work has seen my hands exactly never. I wave, I plead, I perform small jazz routines over the sensor, and it sits there like a bouncer deciding I'm not on the list. Meanwhile the man next to me thinks about water and gets a waterfall.

## Candidate 4 (265 characters)

Every elevator in my building closes its doors with the urgency of a guillotine and opens them with the enthusiasm of a teenager asked to do dishes. I have made eye contact with my own reflection in those closing doors more times than I have made it inside the car.

## Candidate 5 — SELECTED (398 characters)

Every cart I grab has one wheel with a vendetta. It doesn't roll, it argues, squeaking over every conversation and veering at the wine glasses like it's collecting a debt. By the bread aisle I'm not shopping, I'm doing steering correction for a drunk trolley with main-character energy. Statistically, I am the problem — and the wheel knows it, which is why it only wobbles when someone's watching.

## Candidate 6 (243 characters)

My printer waits until the exact moment I need one page — one single page — to announce it is out of cyan, a color I have never asked it to use, for a document that is entirely black text. It is not a printer. It is a toll booth with opinions.

## Candidate 7 (293 characters)

The 'skip intro' button appears for exactly one frame, at a random location, like it's being hunted. I have watched the same fourteen seconds of theme song so many times I could perform it at weddings. Somewhere a UI designer is laughing, and I hope their pillow is warm on both sides forever.

## Candidate 8 (285 characters)

Airport boarding groups make no sense. I am in Group 7, which boards after Group 6, which boards after 'families,' 'status holders,' and apparently anyone who has ever merely seen a plane. By the time my group is called, the overhead bins contain three umbrellas and a sense of defeat.

## Why candidate 5 wins

The shopping-cart wheel is a universal everyday rage trigger. The rant escalates in stages — a wheel with a vendetta, a drunk trolley with main-character energy — then lands a self-own ("Statistically, I am the problem") before the final twist that the wheel only performs when watched. It uses the full character budget without padding.
